In this episode, I interviewed my awesome coach in Mexico- **Mariana Fletcher**!

## About Mariana

Mariana co-founded **[Movement Mexico](https://www.instagram.com/movement__mexico/)** with Jonathan Fletcher in Puerto Escondido, Oaxaca, Mexico. She has a B.A. in Performing Arts. She's a certified yoga teacher under Yoga Works, a certificated Acroyoga teacher and Thai massage practitioner. She has been practicing under the guidance of Ido Portal for 6 years. Her interests also include traditional African dance and is a blue belt in jiujitsu.
